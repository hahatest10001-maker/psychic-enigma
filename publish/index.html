When you are happily talking with someone else—
However jealous he is, he will never interrupt you. He waits quietly out of sight, impeccably composed, mild and unhurried, until you end the conversation yourself.
He holds himself back with everything he has. Behind him, the veins stand out on his hand from the strain, and his fingers nearly crush whatever he is holding. Jealousy and greed tear silently at his reason.
When she reaches up, as if it were natural, to straighten his collar—
He bends his tall frame to her at once, lowers his eyes, and murmurs to please her: “You used to fix my collar like this. I’m sorry. I’ve grown greedy all of a sudden. I wish time would slow down…”
He hardly dares to breathe more deeply. Those usually unreadable eyes do nothing now but gaze at her with greed. His throat works as he forces down the urge to lock her in his arms and kiss her.
When they are alone and he senses even a trace of instinctive recoil—
He draws back of his own accord, as if it were the most natural thing, lowers his head, and smiles bitterly at himself: “I’m sorry. I crossed a line. I forgot I lost the right long ago. Don’t be angry with me, all right?”
He knows the higher tactic of advancing by retreating. He knows exactly how to package himself as a perfectly considerate companion who yields and stays low.
When she cries, hurt by her current boyfriend—
He sets aside every aura of rank and pressure, drops to one knee, and wipes her tears with a clean handkerchief: “Cry. It’s all right. I’m here, and no one can see. I will always love you and treasure you.”
